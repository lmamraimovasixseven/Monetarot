Verdict: the relation is significant at 5% (HC1). The sign is positive: a higher differential is followed by a rise in USD/KZT (a weaker tenge), the opposite of what carry-trade inflows would imply. This is an in-sample association, not a causal estimate and not a forecast; omitted variables such as the oil price, global risk appetite and the rouble can bias it.
