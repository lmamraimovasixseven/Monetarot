The carry-trade mechanism

  1. Real rate: nominal rate − year-on-year inflation.

  2. Partner real rate: a partner's monthly rate is the average over the calendar days of the month, minus its year-on-year inflation (Russia and China; no euro-area inflation is stored).

  3. Lower bound: KZ inflation + the higher of the Russian and Chinese real rates + premium.

  4. Upper bound: KZ inflation + the higher of the Russian and Chinese real rates + cap. The credit constraint is not identified, so the upper bound is the cap only.

  5. Reading: below the corridor: risk of outflows into the carry trade; above the corridor: risk of excessive inflows and harm to credit.
