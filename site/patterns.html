Method decisions

STL window chosen by revisions of the last point

For each window: the mean absolute revision of the seasonally adjusted value of the last point (estimate from data up to t against the estimate from data up to t + 12), over the last 24 origins. The window with the smallest revision is chosen; on a tie, the shorter one.
