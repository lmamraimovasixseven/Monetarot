NBK Base-Rate Decision Forecast

Should a bond manager extend duration before the NBK decision on 4 December 2026?

How hard are six macro factors pushing the National Bank of Kazakhstan towards a rate hike or a cut? Read-only view of a fitted ordered-logit model; nothing is re-estimated here. The forecast is turned into a duration decision with an explicit loss function, and the holdout comparison with Always Hold is shown as prominently as the forecast.

This page is static and fast; there is also an interactive version (Render). Detailed tables are on the Evidence page.

1. The decision

A treasurer or portfolio manager holding tenge government bonds decides, before the NBK meeting on 4 December 2026, whether to extend or shorten duration.

2. The tension

The obvious view: the most likely outcome is a hold (53.2%).

The complication: the probability of a cut (38.7%) has a 95% interval of 4.2%–73.2%, and the 95% band of the pressure score (-0.747 to +0.136) includes zero, so the model cannot tell pressure towards a cut from pressure towards a hike.

Action threshold p* = 0.500 with equal losses; probability of a cut 38.7%.

The loss ratio is an assumption. Move it to see how the threshold changes.

Why these probabilities?
  • Same inputs for both rounds. Both rounds use the latest available factor values (factor data month 2026-08), because future factor values are unknown. Both blocks are therefore the same conditional estimate: the probabilities are identical and only the date differs. This is not an error and not a separate forecast for each date.
  • Why Hold is most likely. Hold was the most common NBK decision in the sample: 49 of 91 decisions in data/processed/nbk_decisions_clean.csv.
  • Hike or cut? The 95% intervals (Increase 0.0%–18.9%, Decrease 4.2%–73.2%) are wide and overlap, and the score band -0.747 to +0.136 includes zero, so the model does not separate a hike from a cut at this date.
  • Holdout comparison. On the 18 holdout decisions the RMSE ratio is 0.995, below 1, so the model meets the pre-specified criterion against Always Hold. This does not show that the model forecasts better. The factors have a detectable link with the decisions (joint likelihood-ratio test p = 0.026). The margin is small; see the Evidence tab for its uncertainty.

6. The risk

Check the source data: an unusually large monthly change enters the latest score (for growth factors, through the 12-month growth rate) — export_history.csv 2026-06 (+56% month on month), modified z = 3.9; import_history.csv 2026-01 (-34% month on month), modified z = -5.0. 7. The ask
