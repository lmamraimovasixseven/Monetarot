## Rebuild conventions and limitations

The authoritative panel rule is the raw level of each of the six factors at decision calendar month minus two. Household lending is rounded to eight decimal places to preserve the existing panel storage precision; this is not a growth/log/difference transform. This reproduces the committed decision panel (`data/processed/nbk_decisions_clean.csv`); `tests/test_data_contract.py` checks that a fresh rebuild equals it. The two-month offset is a reconstruction convention, not a verified publication lag. The prior one-month growth notes for exports and individual lending are obsolete, not used by the current panel, and must not be applied in `build_panel()`.

`data/raw/` files are immutable inputs. No source download, publisher identity, release timing, units, or revision history could be independently verified from the files themselves.
