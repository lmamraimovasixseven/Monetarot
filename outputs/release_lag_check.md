# Release-lag check: assumed vs real

The model aligns every factor to the calendar month 2 months before the decision month (`assumed_release_lag_months` in `config/release_lag.json`). Real lags come from `release_lag_days` in `docs/facts_to_fill.csv`: calendar days from the last day of the reference month to publication. A value counts as available only if it was published strictly before the decision date. Decisions are the usable rows of `data/processed/model_panel.csv`.
