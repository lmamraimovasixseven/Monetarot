# Robustness checks

Total specifications: 10 (one primary specification plus 9 requested robustness specifications). Robustness checks converged: 9; nonconverged or failed: 0.
Each robustness variant uses a chronological 80/20 split within its own usable sample; coefficients are fit on the earlier portion only. All comparisons use Always Hold on the same variant-specific holdout dates. The main holdout and main model are unchanged. `Survives?` means all factor coefficient signs match the primary ordered logit and the variant reaches the same model-versus-Hold RMSE conclusion. These checks are descriptive and are not used to select a final model. NONCONVERGED checks retain their reported diagnostic estimates and scores but should not be treated as successful fits.
For binary logit, the equivalent expected score is P(Increase) × 1 + P(not Increase) × 0, evaluated against the observed ordinal score -1/0/+1.
